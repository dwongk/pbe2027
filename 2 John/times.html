forever(2 John 1:1) The Elder, To the elect lady and her children, whom I love in truth, and not only I, but also all those who have known the truth, (2 John 1:2) because of the truth which abides in us and will be with us forever: (2 John 1:3) Grace, mercy, and peace will be with you from God the Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.
