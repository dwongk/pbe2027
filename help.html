TL;DR

  • This site turns the books for this year's Bible quiz into word lists that are easier to study and remember.
  • Pick a book, then pick a category like people or places, and study the words in it.
  • The number after a word tells you how many times it appears in the book: word/45 means 45 times.
  • Expanded shows the full word lists. Condensed using AI shows the same verses grouped by verse instead.

What this site is

StudyTools is a study aid for Bible quiz competitions. It takes the books assigned for the quiz and turns them into lists of words, sorted by how often each word appears and by what kind of word it is, so the material is easier to learn and recall for a verbal quiz.

The books on this site are the ones assigned for its competition: Bible Bowl Nationals, or the Pathfinder Bible Experience (PBE). Each site uses the Bible version its competition uses.

The two views

Use the View buttons on the home page to switch between the two ways of looking at the same material. Switching views opens a different page, so any book you opened on the home page will close again.

  • Expanded — the full view. Each category page lists every matching word in the book, one row per word, with the verses that contain it.
  • Condensed using AI — the same material grouped by verse. Each row is one verse (or group of verses) with the words from that category that appear in it, and how many times each appears. Three columns: words, counts, verse text.

Browsing a book

On the home page, tap a book name to open it, then tap a category (for example people, places, animals) to see all the matching words and verses from that book. The book name itself is a link to the whole-book page, which shows the entire book with every word highlighted, from beginning to end.

Under Combined Books by Divisions, each division puts its books together, so you can study a whole division at once. Bible Bowl divisions are age groups; PBE 2027 has a single division with all its books.

Category pages

Each category page is a table of words and verses. The words are listed in the order they appear in the book. Words that appear again and again in different places are grouped at the end, rarest first.

Words in the verse text are highlighted in their category's colour. The number after a word tells you how often it appears:

  • word/45 — the word appears 45 times in the book.
  • word>2 — the word appears twice in a row.

A word with no number after it appears once.

The categories

Every word is sorted into one category. The colours match the category links on the home page.

animalsNames of animals
bodypartsParts of the body or personal items
directionsWords indicating direction or compass points
distinctIntangible words, like "faith" or "remember"
foodFood-related words
hitchcockProper names of people and places, from Hitchcock's Bible Names
numbersWords used to describe quantity
objectsAnswers "what" questions
peopleCommon names of people; answers "who" questions
placesCommon names of places; answers "where" questions
reasonsWords answering "why" questions
relationsWords describing relationships
sayingsAnswers "who said" questions
timesAnswers "when" questions
weaponsWords describing weapons or violence
whichAnswers "which" questions
uncatCatch-all for words that fit no other category

Common words such as "the" and "and" are left out entirely, so they do not fill up the uncategorized list.

Search

There is no search box. Use your browser's find function (Ctrl+F / Cmd+F) to locate words or verses on any page.

Finding your way back

Category and book pages have no navigation bar. Use your browser's Back button to return to the home page.
