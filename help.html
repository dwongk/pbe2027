TL;DR

  • This site turns the books for this year's Bible quiz into word lists that are easier to study and remember.
  • Pick a book, then pick a category like people or places, and study the words in it.
  • The number after a word tells you how many times it appears in the book: word/45 means 45 times.
  • Expanded shows the full word lists. In Context shows the same material grouped by verse, and was made mainly for numbers: it turns number words into figures, largest first.

What this site is

StudyTools is a study aid for Bible quiz competitions. It takes the books assigned for the quiz and turns them into lists of words, sorted by how often each word appears and by what kind of word it is, so the material is easier to learn and recall for a verbal quiz.

The books on this site are the ones assigned for its competition: Bible Bowl Nationals, or the Pathfinder Bible Experience (PBE). Each site uses the Bible version its competition uses.

The two views

Use the View buttons on the home page to switch between the two ways of looking at the same material. Switching views opens a different page, so any book you opened on the home page will close again.

  • Expanded - the full view. Each category page lists every matching word in the book, one row per word, with the verses that contain it.
  • In Context - made mainly for the numbers and times categories. It turns number words into figures and lists the largest first, and it pulls in a little of the surrounding words for context. It was applied to every category, but numbers is where it really helps. Like Expanded, it groups the material by verse: each row is one verse (or group of verses) with the words from that category that appear in it.

Browsing a book

On the home page, tap a book name to open it, then tap a category (for example people, places, animals) to see all the matching words and verses from that book. The book name itself is a link to the whole-book page, which shows the entire book with every word highlighted, from beginning to end.

Under Combined Books by Divisions, each division puts its books together, so you can study a whole division at once. Bible Bowl divisions are age groups; PBE 2027 has a single division with all its books.

Category pages

Each category page is a table of words and verses. The words are listed in the order they appear in the book. Words that appear again and again in different places are grouped at the end, rarest first.

Words in the verse text are highlighted in their category's color. The number after a word tells you how often it appears:

  • word/45 - the word appears 45 times in the book.
  • word>2 - the word appears twice in a row.

A word with no number after it appears once.

The categories

The categories exist so you can answer questions: who, what, when, where, why, how, how many, which, whose, and more. That is how the categories were chosen. They give you a different way of looking at the material, instead of just reading it from beginning to end.

For example, to find who said something, open sayings. To find where something happened, open places.

Every word is sorted into one category. The colors match the category links on the home page.

Study tips

  • Start small. Start with the smallest book, then the smallest categories. You do not need to finish a whole category list: study the words that appear 1 to 3 times first, then look over the words that appear most often, which are at the end of the list.
  • Preview the end of each category list first, just to get the big picture, and take note of the most common words.
  • Read as you listen, and listen as you read. Using more senses helps you remember.
  • Remember phrases, not just single names. Learn as much as you can together. For example, instead of just "Claudius Lysias", remember "chief captain Claudius Lysias", so the position comes with the name. Answering with the full phrase also keeps you from losing points to the other team giving a more specific answer.

The Study Tips document (PDF) also has coaching tips for running a team: the 3-out-of-5 rule, using half the questions, switching scores, ranking players, and long practice sessions.

Search

There is no search box. Use your browser's find function (Ctrl+F / Cmd+F) to locate words or verses on any page.

Finding your way back

Category and book pages have no navigation bar. Use your browser's Back button to return to the home page.
