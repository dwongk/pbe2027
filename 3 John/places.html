journey well(3 John 1:5) Beloved, you do faithfully whatever you do for the brethren and for strangers, (3 John 1:6) who have borne witness of your love before the church. If you send them forward on their journey in a manner worthy of God, you will do well, (3 John 1:7) because they went forth for His name’s sake, taking nothing from the Gentiles.
